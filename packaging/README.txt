SkyHUD {{VERSION}}
=================

SkyHUD turns the flight data inside your GoPro and DJI Avata 2 videos into
an animated HUD on its own track in DaVinci Resolve (free or Studio).
The HUD stays editable: it is never burned into your video.

Install
-------
macOS:   double-click "Install SkyHUD.command".
         The first time macOS blocks it (it can't verify the developer):
         click Done, open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
         down and click Open Anyway, then Open Anyway again.
Windows: double-click "Install SkyHUD.bat".
         If SmartScreen appears: More info > Run anyway.

Then restart DaVinci Resolve and open Workspace > Scripts > SkyHUD.

You can delete this folder after installing: SkyHUD keeps its own copy.

The designs folder holds copies of the four built-in designs to start your
own from (see designs/README.txt). The built-in designs are already inside
SkyHUD.

Full guide, usage and making your own designs:
https://github.com/Julianlg752/skyhud (docs/install.md, docs/usage.md, docs/designs.md)
