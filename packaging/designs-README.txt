Example designs
===============

These are copies of SkyHUD's four built-in designs (Goggles copy, Race copy,
Fighter jet copy, Clean copy), to start your own design from or to see how
one is made. The built-in designs are already inside SkyHUD: you don't need
these folders to use them.

To use a copy, put its folder in your designs folder:
  macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/SkyHUD/designs/
  Windows: C:\ProgramData\SkyHUD\designs\
then close and open the SkyHUD panel. It shows up in the Design list.

Each folder holds:
  design.json     name, pieces, places, colors, font (any text editor)
  design.setting  the Fusion tools that draw it

Rename the folder and change "name" in design.json before you share it.
How to edit a design: docs/designs.md
https://github.com/Julianlg752/skyhud/blob/main/docs/designs.md
